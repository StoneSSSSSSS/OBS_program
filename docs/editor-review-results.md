# Independent Editor reviews

Each first-time tester receives one editing brief, a separate running Windows app, private synthetic inputs and the in-app Wiki. The review awards 40 points for completing the edit, 25 for the published result, 15 for saving and preserving sources, 10 for controls and 10 for help. Keep each first result and its later retest separate. The local screenshots, editable projects and exported media live under the ignored `.clipvault-local/qa/editor-reviews` folder.

## Native verification

The dedicated Windows suites check rendered video dimensions and durations, source-frame matches, layered WAV and MP3 audio, trimmed native recordings, fades across splits, subtitle occupancy in a preview and its MP4, rotation, mirroring, cancellation and preservation of existing outputs. Separate resize sweeps cover the editor, expanded monitor and Wiki at three UI scales.

On this Windows machine, a 640 × 360 scrub worker stepping across a two-angle H.264 cut took a median 723.1 ms through Windows composition and 77.8 ms through the direct decoder in eight alternating seeks. The slowest measured seek fell from 1,119.4 to 81.2 ms. Cold startup for a single frame fell from 565.9 to 102.1 ms. Independent decoding identified wide 069 and replay 070/071 on the expected sides of the cut. Rotated, reframed or ambiguously timed sources continue through Windows composition. These measurements cover the media worker, excluding input dispatch and drawing. A separate keyboard-to-screen check alternated comma and period across the cut in two real GUI windows. The median time until a screenshot showed the correct camera fell from 558.5 ms to 218.5 ms across eight inputs. The slowest measured change fell from 728 to 294 ms. These times include the driver and PNG capture.

The native AAC reader previously placed all three cues 1,024 samples (21.33 ms) late. Matching PCM against the original WAV and an independent decoder after the fix gave zero samples of lag in both channels, for full reads, warm seeks and a lossless cut. Three additional Windows tests cover sparse tracks, mono and stereo, a short final packet, gaps and EOF.
